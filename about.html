ABOUT VENTRON

Calculate first.
Then build.

Ventron is an engineering firm that verifies the light distribution of automotive lamps by analysis. We also run forming analysis for outer-panel parts.

Why analysis

Making one automotive part means cutting a mold, pulling a prototype, and fixing whatever goes wrong, then doing it again. Every pass costs time and money. Simulation catches problems earlier and cuts the number of passes.

With a lamp, you cannot tell whether the light distribution meets regulations, or whether it looks the way you intended, until you switch it on. With an outer panel, you do not know where the sheet thins or tears until you press it. Our job is to find that out before you switch it on and before you press it.

We do not hand over a table of numbers or a color map. We tell you what to change, and where.

HOW WE WORK
PRECISION

Prediction, not replay

We aim for analysis that predicts the result before a design change, not analysis that explains it afterwards.

TURNAROUND

Fits your schedule

Scope and speed are adjusted to each development milestone of OEMs and suppliers.

CLARITY

Reports built for decisions

Numbers and color maps are not passed on raw. They are organized so the design team can decide right away.

SCOPE

Focused on optics

Rather than covering many fields thinly, we go deep on lamp optical analysis and extend into outer-panel forming analysis.

TOOLS

SPEOS · AutoForm · CATIA

SPEOS for optical analysis, AutoForm for forming analysis, CATIA for geometry design and review.

R&D

Analysis acceleration tool, in development

Light guide optimization means adjusting the geometry and re-running the analysis dozens of times. We are building an internal tool on the Claude API that suggests the next adjustment for each iteration and drafts the report from the results.
