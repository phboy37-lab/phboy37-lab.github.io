Privacy policy

Effective 2026.10.01 · Ventron

Ventron (“the Company”) handles users’ personal information with care and complies with applicable laws.

1. Personal information collected and how it is collected

The Company does not operate membership registration or online application forms. If you contact us by email (contact@ventron.co.kr), the following information that you provide voluntarily may be collected.

  • Name, email address, phone number (if provided), content of the inquiry

2. Purpose of collection and use

  • Responding to inquiries and project consultations
  • Replying to inquiries and reporting the outcome

3. Retention period

Once an inquiry has been handled, the information is destroyed without delay unless the law provides otherwise. If you ask us to delete it, we destroy it immediately.

4. Provision to third parties

The Company does not provide users’ personal information to outside parties.

5. Cookies and automatic collection tools

This website uses no visitor analytics tools or cookies.

6. Your rights

You may ask at any time to view, correct or delete your personal information. Send the request by email (contact@ventron.co.kr) and we will act without delay.

7. Privacy officer

In charge: Ventron
Email: contact@ventron.co.kr

8. Notice of changes

This policy may change with changes in law or policy. Changes will be announced on this page.
