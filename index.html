What we do

Optical design and SPEOS analysis for automotive lamps. Press-forming analysis for outer panels as a secondary service.

Beam pattern analysis

Ray-traced intensity maps and isocandela curves, checked against the target pattern.

Regulation check

KMVSS and ECE photometric test points, verified at the design stage.

Light guide design

Geometry studies for mood lamps and DRLs, aimed at even luminance along the guide.

Secondary service

Outer-panel forming analysis

Forming limit diagrams (FLD) to locate thinning and fracture risk in press forming.
