AUTOMOTIVE LAMP OPTICS

Automotive lamp optical design and analysis

SPEOS-BASED OPTICAL CAE

We design the optical system of interior mood lamps, DRLs and rear lamps, and analyze its light distribution. Our particular experience is in improving light uniformity by optimizing light guide geometry. Before a prototype exists, we check by simulation whether the design passes regulations and delivers the intended look. Light guide optimization takes many rounds of geometry changes and re-analysis, so we are building an internal tool on the Claude API to speed that up.

  • IN
    Review of specs and design
    We go through the light guide and source specifications and the design intent.
  • SIM
    Light distribution simulation
    Ray tracing computes the beam pattern and the intensity distribution.
  • CHK
    Check against regulations and targets
    We verify compliance with domestic and international regulations.
  • OUT
    Light guide optimization proposal
    A report with geometry changes that improve light uniformity.
DELIVERABLES
  • Beam pattern simulation results, including isocandela curves
  • Regulation compliance review report
  • Light guide optimization proposal
  • Luminance and intensity distribution data, light uniformity analysis
SHEET METAL FORMING · SECONDARY SERVICE

Outer-panel forming analysis

AUTOFORM-BASED STAMPING CAE

We look at thinning, fracture, wrinkling and springback in pressed outer panels by simulation, before the mold exists. We run it alongside a lamp project or on its own.

DELIVERABLES
  • Thickness reduction and forming limit diagram (FLD) results
  • Fracture and wrinkling risk report
  • Springback prediction and compensation values
